# NewTube 1.13.0 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.13.0.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 to 1.12.0 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page
(`poster-1.13.0.html` in the launch folder) and screenshotted with Chrome
(`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no caricature: the subject is a
bare stage with two spotlights and two microphones facing each other, two speech
bubbles (one saying "…", the other answering back with a reply arrow), and a
phone playing a video whose seek bar shows chapter gaps, a "2×" badge and a
comment box under it. It uses the same navy, cream, gold and red palette as the
1.8.0 to 1.12.0 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.13.0", "EDICIÓN FAEMINO Y CANSADO",
"Tú dices una cosa…", "…y yo te contesto otra.", "2×", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Faemino y Cansado and their
deadpan back-and-forth. It is not an endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
