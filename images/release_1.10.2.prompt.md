# NewTube 1.10.2 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.10.2.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was available in the release session
(2026-09-28), so the poster is a typographic composition rendered from a
hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page and screenshotted with Chrome (`agent-chrome`,
`page.set_content`), like the 1.10.1 poster. There is no caricature: the subject
is a tap filling a glass of water, joined by a dotted line to a phone whose
highlighted settings row shows a document, with a paper plane leaving it. It
uses the same navy stage and cream, gold and red lettering palette as the 1.8.0
to 1.10.1 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.10.2", "EDICIÓN TIP Y COLL",
"Se coge el móvil…", "…y se manda el registro.", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No numbers, no claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to the comedy duo Tip y Coll
and the spirit of their step-by-step "cómo llenar un vaso de agua" lessons. It
is not an endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
