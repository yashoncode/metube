# NewTube 1.11.0 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.11.0.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 to 1.10.4 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page and
screenshotted with Chrome (`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no
caricature: the subject is a street of five arched doors, four shut and one open
and lit, with a dotted golden path from the open door down to a phone playing a
video. It uses the same navy, cream, gold and red palette as the 1.8.0 to 1.10.4
posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.11.0", "EDICIÓN TONY LEBLANC",
"Siempre hay una manera de entrar…", "…y los vídeos de los peques, también.",
footer "PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No numbers, no claims, no logos, no
watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Tony Leblanc and the spirit of
his street-smart chancers. It is not an endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
