# NewTube 1.14.0 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.14.0.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 to 1.13.0 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page
(`poster-1.14.0.html` in the launch folder) and screenshotted with Chrome
(`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no caricature: the subject is a
bare stage with two spotlights, a cross under the left one (with a star above
it) and a line under the right one, and between them a phone playing a video
whose seek bar dot sits right under a finger, a dotted trail behind it and two
small click marks above it. It uses the same navy, cream, gold and red palette
as the 1.8.0 to 1.13.0 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.14.0", "EDICIÓN CRUZ Y RAYA",
"¿Y esto qué es, una raya?", "…una raya que te sigue el dedo.", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Cruz y Raya and their
double-act sketches. It is not an endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
