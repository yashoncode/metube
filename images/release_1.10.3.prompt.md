# NewTube 1.10.3 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.10.3.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 and 1.10.2 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page and
screenshotted with Chrome (`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no
caricature: the subject is a red rotary telephone whose cord runs to a phone
showing a settings list, with one ticked row highlighted and a gold pin marking
the spot. It uses the same navy stage and cream, gold and red lettering palette
as the 1.8.0 to 1.10.2 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.10.3", "EDICIÓN MARTES Y TRECE",
"¿Por dónde íbamos?…", "…justo donde lo dejaste.", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No numbers, no claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to the comedy duo Martes y
Trece and the spirit of their phone-in sketches. It is not an endorsement, and
it quotes no real routine.
