# NewTube 1.10.4 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.10.4.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 to 1.10.3 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page and
screenshotted with Chrome (`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no
caricature: the subject is a theatre stage with red curtains drawn back and a
spotlight on a phone whose "Aa" lines grow from small to large above a text-size
slider, with a gold circular update arrow beside it. It uses the same navy stage
and cream, gold and red lettering palette as the 1.8.0 to 1.10.3 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.10.4", "EDICIÓN LINA MORGAN",
"Agradecidos y actualizados…", "…y con la letra a tu tamaño.", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No numbers, no claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Lina Morgan and the spirit of
her curtain calls. It is not an endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
