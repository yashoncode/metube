# NewTube 1.12.0 release artwork

Final asset: `images/release_1.12.0.png` (1080×1350, portrait 4:5).

Mode: no image-generation tool was used. Like the 1.10.1 to 1.11.0 posters, it is
a typographic composition rendered from a hand-written HTML/CSS/SVG page and
screenshotted with Chrome (`agent-chrome`, `page.set_content`). There is no
caricature: the subject is a city street at night with its windows in tidy rows,
a street lamp switched on, a suitcase set down on the pavement, and a phone whose
screen is half light and half dark, playing a video. It uses the same navy, cream,
gold and red palette as the 1.8.0 to 1.11.0 posters.

Text, exactly and only: "NewTube 1.12.0", "EDICIÓN PACO MARTÍNEZ SORIA",
"Cada cosa en su sitio…", "…y con la luz encendida.", footer
"PARODIA · HOMENAJE NO OFICIAL". No numbers, no claims, no logos, no watermark.

It is fictional artwork and an unofficial homage to Paco Martínez Soria and the
spirit of his country folk finding their way around the big city. It is not an
endorsement, and it quotes no real routine.
