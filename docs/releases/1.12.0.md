# NewTube 1.12.0 — Paco Martínez Soria Edition

Release date: **2026-09-30**. Android version code: **11200**. Package:
`io.github.aleixrodriala.arc`. Android 7/API 24 or later; target/compile API 37.

The Spanish announcement is a fictional, unofficial homage to Paco Martínez Soria
and the spirit of his country folk finding their way around the big city. It is
not an endorsement and quotes no real routine.

## Why this release

1.11.0 went public on 29 September and the first Reddit reports came in within a
day: Shorts still showing (and a request to turn them back on), "Only
audio" still shrinking the video into picture-in-picture, Home that stops loading
signed in, an empty Notifications section, the audio language hidden in the
Quality sheet, and the two promised items, "Not interested" (#1) and a light
theme (#8). The owner's calls (30 September): remove Shorts entirely with no
setting, give the audio track its own row and drop Rotate lock, remove
Notifications if YouTube won't serve them, and port "Not interested" if it works
signed in. The comments redesign and a motion round, both started the same day,
ship with them.

## Validation

- Unit tests on the release commit: phone suite (`testStmobileDebugUnitTest`)
  and `common` (`testDebugUnitTest`) with no failures; the MediaServiceCore tests
  of the touched areas (comments, feedback, Shorts flags, Up next continuation)
  pass offline. `PanelApiTest` and `CommentsApiTest.testToggleLike` need a live
  signed-in session and are not run.
- Every branch was checked on an API 35 emulator before merging (details in the
  commit messages) and reviewed by Codex (gpt-6.1-sol); every accepted finding is
  fixed in the commits above. The integration branch had its own Codex review
  (audio pick, PiP recreation, Shorts top-up findings, fixed in `68f3c1a1`
  `fae3caf4` `df4b3e9b`).
- Xiaomi Mi 8 (LineageOS 22.2 / Android 15), Wi-Fi, signed in to a test
  account, side-by-side benchmark build of `integrate/0930`:
  - Notifications: every inbox attempt refused (TV 400 "Precondition check
    failed", WEB 400 "invalid argument"), RSS fallback empty; so the section is
    removed.
  - Not interested and Don't recommend channel: YouTube answered `code=200
    processed=true`, the cards left Home.
  - Home kept paging past its first shelves (`shelf-tail page` up to 171 cards
    in one session); a page of the Shorts shelf lands empty (one wasted
    request, nothing shown).
  - After the motion merge (`d80e8659`): cold start shows the shimmer skeleton,
    the open morph shows Home behind it in light and dark (no blank frames),
    Back minimizes, controls appear on the first tap, the gear sheet lists
    Quality / Subtitles / Playback speed / Picture-in-picture / More with no
    Rotate lock, a sideways swipe closes the mini player and releases the
    player, the comments panel opens under the video.
- Light theme: 27 screens compared as before/after-dark/light triptychs on the
  emulator (the dark side pixel-identical to the dark-only build), then Home,
  the player, sheets and Settings in both themes on the Mi 8.
- Pixel 9 (Android 17): the owner used release builds of the motion branch on
  his daily app through the day (`4efe977c`'s tree equals `d80e8659`'s: the same
  code as this release apart from the update-check fix); the comments panel
  timed at 0-1 janky frames per action at 120 Hz.
- Update check: API 35 emulator with a local manifest. First launch checks 3 s
  after Home appears and the splash's call is skipped; a return within the hour
  sends nothing; with the clock two hours ahead and the same process, a return
  checks and the You tab shows "Update available" and its dot. After the Codex
  (sol) review's two fixes: an airplane-mode launch sends nothing and shows no
  toast; back online, a return finds the next test version (with the last answer
  stamped in the future after a clock change).
- Not tested: a real Samsung or Android 7 device, LTE, picture-in-picture
  window contents on a real phone (emulator screenshots show it black), the
  grid sections' paging past all-Shorts pages signed in (unit-tested).

## Known issues

- Search with the keyboard open and a mini player docked leaves spare space at
  the bottom.
- Light theme on Android 11 and older: the launch splash follows the system
  theme when the app's choice is the opposite one.
- 1.11.0 installs find this update with the old rule (a new launch, 12 h after
  the last check) or through Check for updates.
