## Why this release

Two rounds the owner tried on his own Pixel the night 1.13.0 shipped: the seek
bar was "very very difficult to drag back to the beginning", so it was rebuilt
on YouTube's measured relative drag ("it's insanely good"), and the player's
drags got Pixel-style haptics ("now it's amazing, we can merge"). Both were
merged to main before midnight; this release ships them with the About rows
and a channel-tab fix.

## Validation

- Seek bar: YouTube 21.18 measured on the reference emulator (relative drag,
  slop, gain, cancel behaviour) and the owner's drag strokes captured on his
  Pixel; unit tests green (782) and emulator scenarios on the branch; Codex
  (gpt-6.1-sol) review findings fixed in `34449659`; on the owner's Pixel since
  10-01 00:37.
- Haptics: the Pixel 9's own vibration vocabulary measured from the system
  (vibrator history while the owner did the gestures); every path checked on
  an emulator through its vibrator history; four rounds on the owner's Pixel;
  Codex (gpt-6.1-sol) review, 5 findings, all fixed in `bfa948e0`.
- Release commit: `assembleStmobileDebug` builds.
- Not tested: a real Samsung or Android 7 device; haptics on a phone without
  vibration primitives beyond the fallback path (the Mi 8 has none).

## Known issues

- Search with the keyboard open and a mini player docked leaves spare space at
  the bottom.
- Light theme on Android 11 and older: the launch splash follows the system
  theme when the app's choice is the opposite one.

## Distribution

Release for the owner to publish. Same package and signing certificate as
1.13.0, so it upgrades in place; do not uninstall or clear data. 1.12.0 and
later installs find it by themselves within an hour of coming back to Home;
older ones through Check for updates. Publishing posts it to Discord
