# NewTube 1.10.0 — tagged, not distributed

Tag `v1.10.0` (`c22968c`, 2026-09-26), versionCode 11000. It was built, signed,
installed on the owner's Pixel 9 and tagged, but never sent to the tester group.

On the owner's Android 17 phone its headline mini-player change - X parks the
video and leaves a paused notification to resume it for 10 minutes - showed no
notification and no lock-screen player after X. The app did post the paused
media notification (`vis=PUBLIC`, `category=transport`, `NO_CLEAR`, session
PAUSED), but SystemUI's media pipeline logged it entering and leaving at once
(`MediaLog: media notification entered pipeline` / `exited pipeline`), and the
media carousel stayed empty. The earlier Pixel passes had only checked
`dumpsys media_session` and `cmd media_session dispatch`, not a visible
notification. The cause was not established; the owner chose to drop the
feature.

**1.10.1** is 1.10.0 without the park: see [its record](1.10.1.md). The park
code stays in history (`8b6e310`, branch `feature/miniplayer-resume`) and in
HANDOFF §31 for a future attempt.
