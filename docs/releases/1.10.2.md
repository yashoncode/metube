## Why this release

On 27 September, minutes after 1.10.1 went to the tester group, a tester
reported "Unknown source error" on every video after a while of playback. The
phone was a Redmi Note 14 4G (MediaTek Helio G99) running HyperOS 3,
`OS3.0.302.0.WOGEUXM`, Android 16 `BP2A.250605.031.A3`. Their screenshot shows a
caption cue under the error, so captions were on and the video had been
playing.

That message means 4 consecutive source errors with a server verdict on a
validated network. It is not the no-connection path. None of these reproduced
it on an Android 16 emulator: the exact 1.10.1 ARM64 APK installed in place, a
clean real 1.9.0 upgraded to 1.10.1, or about 3 minutes with Catalan captions
on. All three ran signed out on Wi-Fi. Nothing public matched the device or
HyperOS 3.

Testers could not send a log without adb. 1.10.2 adds that, so the next report
names the failing request.

## How the diagnostic log works

- One `logcat -v threadtime` child starts 3 s after launch, in the main process
  only. An app without READ_LOGS reads only its own uid, so the stream is this
  app and nothing else. It includes the previous process's lines still in the
  device buffer, which covers a force-close before sending. The last
  8000 lines, and at most 1.5 M characters, are kept in memory. On a restart
  the reader resumes with `-T` from its last timestamp. If the reader never saw
  a real log line, the export takes a one-shot `logcat -d` with a 10 s
  watchdog.
- The export writes `cacheDir/diagnostics/newtube-diagnostics-<time>.txt`,
  keeps reports younger than an hour so an earlier share is not pulled out from
  under a chat app, and shares it through the existing FileProvider
  (`${applicationId}.update_provider`, cache path). Nothing leaves the phone
  unless the user picks a share target.
- `DiagnosticRedactor` removes OAuth access and refresh tokens, Authorization
  and SAPISIDHASH values, JWTs, cookies, e-mail addresses, PO tokens, visitor
  data and identifiers, device codes and client secrets (including spaced
  labels), proxy `user:password@`, and account-name JSON fields. In URLs it
  handles any case, `socks`/`ws`/`ftp` schemes, JSON-escaped and percent-encoded
  forms, and path-style googlevideo parameters. Every query value is removed
  except a media whitelist: itag, mime, clen, dur, range, expire, lmt, c, cver,
  video ids and the like. The client IP never survives. Short flag values (`pot=n`,
  `key=n`) are kept on purpose.
- The About subtitle promises only what the code guarantees: the log lists the
  videos and channels opened, and passwords and sign-in tokens are removed.

## Validation and limitations

- Unit tests on the release tree: **633 in `smarttubetv`** (1 opt-in benchmark
  skipped) and **258 in `common`** (238 + 20 new redactor/ring tests) passed.
- Cross-model review (Codex `gpt-6-sol`, high effort, adversarial). It raised
  eight findings: spaced device-code/client-secret labels, proxy credentials,
  chat text and channel titles, uppercase/encoded URLs and other Authorization
  schemes, logged media error bodies, a second share deleting the first file,
  logcat stderr counting as a log plus no dump timeout, and URL over-truncation.
  All are fixed except the ones listed as known gaps below.
- Android 16 emulator (API 36), debug build: the recorder runs as a `logcat`
  child of the app process. A 3,780-line export spanned three app sessions,
  including the earlier 1.10.1 release process, with **zero** matches for
  OAuth-token, Bearer, e-mail, cookie, `ip=`, `poToken=`, `identifier=` and
  over-redacted video-id patterns. Two shares in a row kept both files.
- Android 16 emulator, the signed 1.10.2 ARM64 release installed in place:
  video picture visible in ~1.5 s, About shows 1.10.2, and the button opens
  the share sheet with the report.
- Signed release assembly passed. For every APK, `apksigner` shows the release
  certificate and `aapt2` shows versionCode 11002, versionName 1.10.2, not
  debuggable. The ARM64 APK passes `zipalign -c -P 16`.
- Pixel 9 (Android 17): in-place upgrade of 1.10.1 to the exact signed ARM64
  artifact (`versionCode=11002`, not debuggable, app data kept). It landed
  `[status=speed-profile] [reason=install]` from the embedded profile. No
  playback timing was redone: the release changes no player code.
- Known gaps: the redactor works line by line, so a secret split across two log
  lines is not recognised. Up to 240 printable bytes of a googlevideo error
  response stay in the NetPath error line; these are generic YouTube error
  pages. Channel and video titles are kept by design. Some OEM ROMs may
  refuse the `logcat` child; the report then says so in its Recorder line. The
  "Unknown source error" report itself is **not** fixed in this release.
