## Why this release

1.10.4 left made-for-kids videos stopping with "Unknown source error" (#5)
because the fix needed more than a hotfix. The way NewTube chooses a YouTube
source for a video was redesigned from benchmarks instead of patched: one
planner for signed-out and signed-in playback, stop rules for videos that
cannot play, and account-route health in place of a stack of phone-only gates
(design 3, `docs/player-sources/LANES.md`). The time to first frame was worked
on at the same time, and the in-app update flow held back from 1.10.4 ships
with it.

On 29 September two testers reported the other "Unknown source error": the
video plays about a minute, then stops. Round r11 played long on real phones
and found why: for some anonymous identities (1 in 17 over the Pixel's LTE, 3
in 14 on home Wi-Fi that day) googlevideo serves the first 60 s of media to
VISIONOS and ANDROID_VR and refuses everything past it, also after a seek or
a resume. The previous code's recovery only reached sources that were refused
too. The release remembers that refusal, recovers through sources that keep
serving (WEB_EMBED, TV_TIZEN, ANDROID_REEL) and rolls a fresh playback
identity (router v22, LANES §9).

## Validation and limitations

- Unit tests on the merged release tree (29 September evening): **664 in
  `smarttubetv`** (1 opt-in benchmark skipped), **270 in `common`**, and **415**
  in `youtubeapi`'s player-source package (`videoinfo.V2`: planner, walk
  replay, wall memory, identity re-roll, recent refusals, bot wall; 3 skipped:
  two replay cases of deliberately changed behaviour and one opt-in), all
  passing; `appupdatechecker2` (17) is unchanged since the update-flow run. The
  walk replay has 22 cases, including the real walled visitor on an
  embedding-disabled video (`walled-visitor-no-embed`).
- Devices (29 September): a Pixel 9 (Android 17) signed out over LTE and home
  Wi-Fi and signed in over Wi-Fi with the owner's account on a benchmark build
  that blocks every watch-history write; a Xiaomi Mi 8 (LineageOS 22.2 /
  Android 15, Snapdragon 845) signed out over Wi-Fi; an x86_64 emulator signed
  in. Results in `docs/player-sources/LANES.md` §7:
  - kids videos: 25/25 over LTE at the second request (median first frame
    1.2-1.3 s), where 1.10.4 failed or took 8-10 s;
  - signed in: 13/16 of a 16-video ring (the other three cannot play) in 40
    requests; the previous code, 3 of 7 in 128;
  - videos that cannot play settle in 2-4 requests (removed 4, 18+ not
    embeddable 2, members-only 3-4), paid and music-only videos in 9 (YouTube's
    generic refusal);
  - normal cold starts, median picture visible: Pixel kids 745 ms, ordinary
    529 ms; Mi 8 kids 1162 ms, ordinary 822 ms; switching videos on the Mi 8
    about 0.4 s faster with the V8 memo;
  - a fresh install's first ordinary/live video: Pixel 0.79/1.20 s against
    1.93/2.51 s, Mi 8 0.93/1.29 s against 3.70/3.98 s.
- Round r11 (29 September afternoon and evening, long plays and everyday
  start times; `~/projects/newtube-launch/netbench/r11-*.md`):
  - the one-minute wall reproduced on the previous code (v10, planner off:
    VISIONOS walled at 60.0 s, then ANDROID_VR walled, then the reload cap and
    "Unknown source error"). It follows the anonymous identity, not the
    build and not the playback pings (walls with pings on and off; a walled
    identity stayed walled 33 min and refused a skip past 60 s at once);
  - on two real walled identities (emulators, home IP) the previous release
    candidate v19 recovered 6 of 7 walled videos through WEB_EMBED and failed
    the embedding-disabled one; v22 recovered it through TV_TIZEN and the
    fresh playback identity played 4 of 4 new videos past 60 s;
  - v22b with a synthetic wall (`debug.arc.poison_wall_s=60`) on the Mi 8
    (Wi-Fi) and the Pixel (Wi-Fi and LTE): the embedding-disabled video
    recovered in 0.84-1.22 s (the resume goes back about 6 s; on the real walls
    on the slower emulators, 4.2 and 11.5 s), and the next
    videos played past 60 s on the fresh identity at normal start times;
  - long plays, 300 s each: v19 9/9 signed out and 9/9 signed in over the
    Pixel's LTE, 9/9 on the Mi 8 (and 3/3 with captions on); v22 9/9 on the
    Mi 8; no error, no media 403, no reload cap;
  - everyday cold starts on the Mi 8, median picture visible, v22b against
    v10 (the closest to 1.10.4): ordinary 549 against 800 ms, kids 1055
    against 8346, 18+ about 1.1 s against 3.8 s without a pre-roll, live 1067
    against 1032 (a watch item). On the Pixel the frame lift took ordinary
    cold starts from 684 to 562 ms and kids from 998 to 764 ms; the warm-up
    hold that gains 178 ms on the Mi 8 was flat to +50 ms there, all of it
    before YouTube's answer, where the hold cannot act (network noise).
- Update flow: end to end on the Pixel's signed-in daily app (owner's OK) with
  test builds 1.10.5 → 1.10.6 and 1.10.7 → 1.10.8 served locally: the You-tab
  dot and row, the sheet (version, size, notes), download, the one-time install
  permission, Android's installer and the "Updated to" snackbar. It found the
  "Open with" chooser (three apps on that phone also open APK files), fixed in
  `bfabdb2c` and re-verified.
- Cross-model reviews (Codex): the lane design (astra, reconciled in LANES
  §10), the private-video fix, the player-JS gate, the walk replay, the V8
  solver lane and the kids channel memory (sol); every accepted finding is
  fixed in the commits above.
- YouTube walled the Pixel's carrier IP once during the LTE runs (explicit bot
  text from three sources). The runs stopped there; Wi-Fi continued.
- Watch: on the Mi 8, cold ordinary opens measured 822 ms median with the V8
  memo against 744 ms without (n=3, overlapping ranges).
- Wide videos (#9): checked on an emulator at the reporter's 1220x2712 screen
  (fullscreen, pinch to fill, rotation, five mini-card park cycles, hops between
  shapes while minimized) and on a Pixel 7 emulator profile with a real cutout.
  A hop while minimized still shows the card black for 1-3 frames at the switch;
  1.10.4 does the same (6/6 hops on both builds), so it is a separate item.
- Not tested: a real Samsung or Android 7 device; LTE since the V8 lane.
